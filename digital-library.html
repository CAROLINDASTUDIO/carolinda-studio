FEATURED STORY

The Whale

A story about dreams, adventure and a mysterious connection beneath the ocean.

Some stories begin with a dream.

Some dreams feel so real that, when we wake, we cannot simply forget them.

The Whale begins with such a dream — and with a fourteen-year-old boy who discovers a mysterious connection to an extraordinary creature beneath the ocean.

THE WHALE

Beneath the Ocean

The depth and vastness of the ocean were unbelievably beautiful.

So beautiful that they completely took my breath away. My heartbeat quickened. It pounded hard and fast against my chest, and a tingling sensation spread through my body — a mixture of comfort, excitement and pure euphoria.

It felt like butterflies fluttering through my limbs, stronger than anything I had ever experienced before.

It was joy.

Pure joy.

I was simply happy and carefree.

I no longer thought about anything. I forgot the time. I forgot every bit of reason. I forgot everything that existed between reality and illusion.

I even forgot who I was.

I no longer knew my own name.

Here and now, in this breathtaking moment, nothing needed to make sense.

I was simply happy.

More than happy.

And suddenly I heard it.

The call.

A breathtaking, deep call that made my entire body tremble.

I felt electrified from head to toe, and a wave of excitement and euphoria rushed through me.

The call was the call of a whale.

My whale.

The gigantic creature that left anyone speechless was slowly moving through the depths of the ocean toward me.

Directly toward me.

I almost felt as though it could look through its enormous, azure-blue whale eyes into my small, dark-brown human eyes.

And then, flowing through the joy of being alive, came a sense of adventure.

I wanted to reach my whale.

Now.

I kicked with my human feet, stretched out my arms and swam toward it at such incredible speed that it almost seemed unreal.

I swam closer and closer.

And when I was almost close enough to touch its enormous whale nose with my tiny human hand...

suddenly, I heard music.

I was so confused that I could not understand anything anymore.

And suddenly my whale blurred before me.

It became transparent and suddenly seemed impossibly far away.

In an instant, panic took over.

The joy disappeared inside me.

My whale had to stay with me.

The music continued to thunder through my ears, but I could make no sense of it.

There was only one thought in my mind:

My whale.

It was moving farther and farther away.

I wanted to scream, but I was underwater, which made my body feel as though it might explode.

So I screamed inside for so long that suddenly I woke up and came crashing back into reality.

I was drenched in sweat.

And I could still hear the music.

It was my alarm clock.

My heart was pounding in my throat, and I realized, bitterly disappointed, that I had only been dreaming.

I breathed out shakily and then tried to breathe slowly again.

In and out.

Slowly, I felt my racing heartbeat calm down.

Then I quickly turned off the alarm.

My eyes fell on the long, large window of my room. As always, I could see the enormous skyscrapers of Los Angeles, their surfaces glowing in the warm sunlight.

I had known this view for a long time.

With a groan, I let myself fall back onto my warm bed, pulled my stuffed animal — a snow leopard — back toward me and hugged it tightly against my chest.

I could feel the soft fur of its head beneath my neck.

I knew that at fourteen I was probably far too old for a stuffed animal.

But I needed it.

Besides, apart from my mom and my older sister, nobody else knew about it.

So it could stay our little secret.

Then I remembered that I had to go to school today.

But what was I even doing at school?

I did not belong there.

I belonged...

In the ocean.

Immediately, images from my dream appeared in my mind: the endless beauty of the colossal ocean, the wonderful silence of the sea and...

the whale.

Its breathtaking, deep call.

And those azure-blue whale eyes that had looked at me.

I could still see its extraordinary gaze perfectly clearly.

Its enormous eyes had been filled with love, confidence and something almost magical.

But there had also been a certain urgency in them when I had swum closer and closer...

and finally woken up.

Now my eyes stared at the ceiling.

The chewing gum was still stuck there — the one I had once thrown onto the ceiling in frustration over my homework.

It made me smile for a moment as I remembered what had happened back then.

Although it had not been funny at the time, somehow it was now.

The chewing gum had also distracted me slightly from my dream, and I slowly realized how motionless I had been lying in bed.

With a sigh, I slipped out of my warm bed, stood up and hopped over to my wardrobe, which looked as though it might collapse at any moment.

I quickly grabbed a pair of ripped blue jeans and a long white shirt.

I pulled them on and ran my fingers through my messy hair.

Then I slung my backpack over my shoulder and set off through Los Angeles on my way to school.

Along the way, my thoughts returned to my dream.

And suddenly, I realized something.

Even though there was no logical explanation, my heart was telling me very clearly that it mattered.

This dream had to have a meaning.

Just like the whale.

I could still clearly feel the incredibly strong connection that had existed between us.

My heartbeat quickened again as I realized:

Because of my whale, my life was about to change completely.
