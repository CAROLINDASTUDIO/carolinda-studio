MY FIRST BOOK

A new chapter begins.

There are moments in life when we believe we know where we are going.

We make plans.
We build a career.
We create a family.
We dream about the future.

And then, sometimes, life changes the direction.

Not because we have failed.

Not because our dreams were wrong.

But because there is something new waiting for us.

This is a story about such a journey.

It is my story — but it is also a story about something much bigger than one person.

It is about courage when life becomes difficult.

It is about faith when the answers do not come.

It is about motherhood, dreams, beauty, work, loss, hope and the quiet strength that can grow inside us when we have no choice but to keep going.

For many years, I believed that being strong meant carrying everything.

Today, I understand strength differently.

Sometimes strength means saying enough.

Sometimes it means asking for help.

Sometimes it means letting go of the life you thought you had to live.

And sometimes it means having the courage to begin again.

I have learned that a new beginning does not always arrive with a dramatic moment.

Sometimes it begins quietly.

With a thought.

With a prayer.

With one small decision:

I want something different for my life.

This book is about that decision.

It is about the road that brought me here — and the road that is still ahead.

I do not know exactly where it will lead.

But I have learned something important:

We do not need to see the entire path to take the next step.

We only need enough faith to walk forward.

So this is not a book about having all the answers.

It is a book about continuing to believe.

About finding beauty again.

About discovering that our story is not finished simply because one chapter has ended.

Perhaps you are standing at the beginning of your own new chapter.

If so, I hope these pages remind you of something:

You are not too late.
Your dreams are not too old.Your life is not finished.

And sometimes, the most beautiful chapters are the ones we have not written yet.

This is my first book.

And perhaps, in some small way, it can become a new beginning for you, too.

The journey has begun
